# Gameplay

How to play DEADZONE.

## Objective

Survive waves of zombies. Kill enemies to gain XP, level up, and unlock upgrades. Reach the highest wave possible.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| W / Up Arrow | Move up |
| S / Down Arrow | Move down |
| A / Left Arrow | Move left |
| D / Right Arrow | Move right |
| SPACE | Screen clear bomb (kills all enemies) |
| F | Freeze (stops all enemies for 3 seconds) |
| ESC / Q | Quit game |

## Combat

Weapons fire automatically at the nearest enemy. No manual aiming required. Focus on positioning and dodging.

## Enemy Types

| Type | Symbol | Speed | Health | XP |
|------|--------|-------|--------|-----|
| Zombie | z | Slow | 10 | 10 |
| Runner | r | Fast | 5 | 15 |
| Tank | Z | Slow | 30 | 50 |

All enemies move toward the player. Runners are fragile but fast. Tanks are slow but durable.

## Boss

A boss spawns at wave 10. It has 500 HP, takes up multiple characters on screen, and becomes enraged at 50% health.

## Leveling

Killing enemies gives XP. When you level up:
1. Health is fully restored
2. Max HP increases by 10
3. Choose one of three random upgrades

### Upgrades

- **Max Health+**: Increase maximum HP
- **Move Speed+**: Move faster
- **Damage+**: Deal more damage per hit
- **Fire Rate+**: Shoot faster
- **Bullet Speed+**: Projectiles travel faster
- **Multi-Shot+**: Fire additional projectiles

Each upgrade can be leveled up to 5 times.

## Abilities

Two special abilities with cooldowns:

### Screen Clear Bomb (SPACE)
- Kills all enemies on screen instantly
- Creates explosion effects at each enemy position
- 20 second cooldown
- Use when overwhelmed

### Freeze (F)
- Freezes all enemies in place for 3 seconds
- Enemies cannot move or attack while frozen
- 15 second cooldown
- Good for repositioning or focused kills

The HUD shows ability status:
- `READY` = Available to use
- `Xs` = Cooldown remaining (X seconds)
- `ACTIVE!` = Freeze currently active

## Waves

Each wave spawns a set number of enemies. Kill them all to advance. Higher waves spawn:
- More enemies
- Faster enemies
- Tougher enemy types

## Scoring

Your score is based on:
- Enemies killed
- Wave reached
- Player level

High scores are saved automatically and displayed on the game over screen.

## Tips

1. Keep moving. Standing still gets you surrounded.
2. Save the bomb for emergencies.
3. Use freeze to safely clear clusters.
4. Prioritize runners - they're fast and dangerous.
5. Stay near the center to have escape routes in all directions.
